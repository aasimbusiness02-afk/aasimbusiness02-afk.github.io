Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Video Factory ("the app") is a private tool operated by a single owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and publishing times, and read basic information about the owner's own channels and videos.
  • With the owner's permission, the app uses the Instagram and Facebook Graph APIs to publish videos to the owner's own accounts and Pages.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about viewers or any other people.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

The owner can delete all stored tokens by removing them from the computer and revoking access in the Google and Meta account settings.
